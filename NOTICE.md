# Attribution and distribution

This research reproduction package accompanies *Estimating Operator Fatigue
During Manual Assembly with a Recursive State-Space Model of Wearable
Eye-Tracking Signals* by Margaret Bowen, Shengbo Kang, Leonardo Venturoso,
Isabella Soraruf, Christoph Huber-Huber, and Marco Todescato.

The audited offline estimator and complete-bundle module were extracted from
the Fraunhofer Italia eye-tracking toolkit. Their source bytes are preserved;
the original toolkit developers are Leonardo Venturoso and Isabella Soraruf.
Their attribution is retained here. See `provenance/` for source hashes and the
numerical audit.
